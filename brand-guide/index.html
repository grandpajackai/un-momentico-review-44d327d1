The mark

A window that makes the light kinder

In old Cuban houses, a medio punto sits above the inner doors: a half-circle fan of coloured glass set in wood. Alejo Carpentier called it “un enorme abanico de cristales abierto sobre la puerta interior, el patio, el vestíbulo”, and wrote that the medio punto was “el intérprete entre el Sol y el Hombre”: the go-between that turned a harsh sun into something a family could live with.

That is exactly what a session with Danny is for. You bring something that feels too bright or too heavy to look at, and you leave with it softened, coloured, easier to hold. The fan mark is that window. Its panes are the colours of Havana glass (monte green, amber, guava, sage) and each of Danny’s four kinds of session owns one of them.

The fan is also a clock face cut in half, open at the top. It never closes. Your session ends when you’re ready, not when the clock says.

Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the height of the fan’s inner hub (x, about a third of the fan height) on every side. Nothing else enters that box.

Minimum size

Mark alone: 24 px wide on screen, 7 mm in print. Lockup: 140 px or 32 mm wide. Below that, use the mark alone. Drop the “Coaching Group with Danny Cabrera” line below 200 px.

Voice and tone

Like a tío who listens before he speaks

Warm, not sugary

Talk to one person. Short sentences. Room to breathe.

Honest about limits

Never promise a message, an outcome or a healing. Say what a session is and what it isn’t.

Faith-forward, door-open

Danny’s faith shows; nobody else is asked to share it.

Bilingual like home

Spanish is not a translation. Write it the way a Cuban family in Hialeah actually talks: momentico, abuela, con cariño.

Photography

Real, warm, close

Only real photographs of Danny, taken with his consent. Natural window light, warm and slightly desaturated, on sand or limewash backdrops. Frame him in arches and doorways where possible. No photos of his children, no AI-generated people, no stock “spiritual” clichés (candles, crystal balls, glowing hands, galaxies).

  • Shot list for a future session: Danny at a real doorway or arch in Miami Lakes or Little Havana; hands around a cafecito; listening, mid-conversation (with a consenting adult, face turned away); walking, unposed.
  • Grade: lift shadows slightly, warm the whites toward cal (#EAE2D3), pull saturation 10 to 15%.

Tiles and pattern

One pane per way in

Each kind of session is a single wedge of the fan, in its own glass colour, with a fine diagonal texture like hand-cut glass. Wedges light up (full opacity plus a warm glow) when they are in focus.

  • Spiritual guidanceGuía espiritual#2F5D50
  • Messages from loved onesMensajes de tus seres queridos#C47C6E
  • Life purpose coachingCoaching de propósito de vida#C99A5B
  • Family sessionsSesiones en familia#9DBDB0

Pattern: repeating fans at 12% opacity in oro light on monte. Use for backgrounds of dark bands, never behind body text.

Layout and motion

One focal point, plenty of patio

Grid

12 columns, max 1360 px, fluid gutters. Asymmetric splits (4 + 7, 5 + 6) rather than centred stacks.

Focal point

Each screen has one thing to look at first: a big Caslon line, the door portrait or the clock. Supporting text is smaller, lighter and quieter.

Space

Sections breathe: 64 to 140 px vertical padding. Tinted bands (papel, monte) separate ideas instead of boxes and borders.

Motion is light, not movement

Things glow rather than fly: panes light up in sequence, tiles brighten in view, the clock hand drifts past sixty. Reveals travel 18 px at most. Everything stops under prefers-reduced-motion.
