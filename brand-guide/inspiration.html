Where the light comes from

Source credits and links behind the visual direction. Third-party mood thumbnails are omitted in this review copy; all original brand marks, glass tiles, and portraits are local.

1

Medio punto and vitrales

  • Ref

    Luz y color, la magia de los vitrales de Cuba

    Diario de Cuba, Jan 2023

    A classic medio punto: green and violet petals radiating from a hub, wood leading. The direct ancestor of our fan mark. The article covers embellotado (glass set in wood with putty) and glass imported from Bohemia.

    Photo: Diario de Cuba (article lead image). Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).

  • Ref

    El arte del vitral en la arquitectura cubana

    Havana 24/7

    Rose-window symmetry and the deep blue we deliberately left out: our palette keeps the greens, ambers and guava, which read warmer on limewash.

    Image: Havana 24/7 (og:image). Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).

  • Ref

    Vitrales para andar La Habana (+ Fotos)

    Juventud Rebelde, José Luis Estrada Betancourt, 28 Sep 2019

    A walking route through the stained glass of Rosa María de la Terga Tabío (1935 to 2017), from San Francisco de Paula to Café del Oriente. Tall lit glass in a dark room: the contrast we use on the hero band.

    Photo: Juventud Rebelde. Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).

  • Ref

    Vitrales para andar La Habana (+ Fotos)

    Juventud Rebelde, 28 Sep 2019

    Glass inside a working room, with people in it. Light that serves a place, not a museum piece.

    Photo: Juventud Rebelde. Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).

  • Ref

    Vitrales para andar La Habana (+ Fotos)

    Juventud Rebelde, 28 Sep 2019

    A lucernario (skylight) panel: framed, symmetrical, ornamental. Reference for the border rule in our tiles.

    Photo: Juventud Rebelde. Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).

  • Ref

    Vitrales habaneros, luces que proyectan historia

    Trabajadores, Yimel Díaz Malmierca, 14 Mar 2025

    Quotes Rosa de la Terga: “La vitralería colonial cubana, siempre espléndida en el mediopunto… es única en el mundo”, Carpentier’s “enorme abanico de cristales”, and Eusebio Leal’s “luz que proyecta la historia”. Glass was there to soften the sun and the rain.

    Text source (no image used). Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).

  • Ref

    El mediopunto cubano, de La Habana a Extremadura

    Hoy (blog Guiris por Extremadura), 1 Oct 2013

    Quotes Alejo Carpentier, La ciudad de las columnas: “el medio punto cubano haya sido el intérprete entre el Sol y el Hombre.” The heart of our brand story: glass that turns harsh light into something kind.

    Text source (no image used). Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).

  • Ref

    Arcos, columnas y portales definen el rostro de La Habana

    CiberCuba, Aug 2019

    Portales and round arches: the half-circle as a doorway between street and home. Why every section opens with an arch or a fan.

    Photo: CiberCuba. Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).

  • Ref

    Old Havana and its Fortification System

    UNESCO World Heritage Centre

    Colour and weathered limewash at city scale. Palette check for cal, arena and monte.

    Image: UNESCO World Heritage Centre (og:image). Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).

  • Ref

    Trinidad and the Valley de los Ingenios

    UNESCO World Heritage Centre

    Ochre walls, green shutters, late sun. The warmth we want in photography grading.

    Image: UNESCO World Heritage Centre (og:image). Reference link and attribution only (image omitted in public review copy).
