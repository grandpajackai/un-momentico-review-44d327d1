## 1. Brand Pillars (Personal Brand — Separate From Med Group)

These are the themes the brand and content are built on. Confirm with Danny before treating them as final.

- **1. A life of listening.** Years of answering calls when someone needed a calm voice, into this calling. The listening did not change. Background details (public safety, service, the move into coaching) stay **to confirm**.
- **2. Life purpose and God-given gifts.** Life Purpose Coach work: helping people notice the gifts already placed in them. Prefer his words: “Knowing that we all have God given gifts.”
- **3. Connection with loved ones.** **Confirm with Danny.** Spiritual guidance and mediumship — including messages from loved ones who have passed, when that gift is present. Held with humility. No guaranteed messages or outcomes.
- **4. Un momentico — bilingual, Cuban-American hospitality.** The name is the invitation to pause. A Cuban-American coach in South Florida, at home in English and Spanish.
- **5. Faith as his story, not your requirement.** His Catholic faith is personal story and motivation, not a condition of sitting with him. All faiths, and none, are welcome if they arrive open.

### Brand guardrails (principles, not pillars)
- **Open to every faith.** Warm spiritual guidance for people of all faiths and none, as long as they arrive open.
- **Grief and loved ones.** **Confirm with Danny** whether this framing fits how he wants to talk about the work, and how forward it should sit.
- **It ends when you're ready.** A supporting dimension of how a session feels — not the lead message. Sessions are scheduled for one hour, with buffer time. Proposed signature: *“Your session ends when you're ready, not when the clock says.”*
- **Clear boundaries.** For spiritual and personal insight — not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice. Kept strictly separate from any medical or clinical practice.

Legal entity for this work: the active **Un Momentico LLC**.
