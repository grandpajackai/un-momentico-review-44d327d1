3. 30 / 60 / 90-Day Schedule

Days 1–30 · Foundation and Launch

  • Fix up and set up every platform, not only Instagram: Instagram (bio, category, booking link, highlights), LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, X, and Google Business Profile if he sees people in person. Proposed IG bio (children’s names omitted unless he asks to keep them): Husband, father & Life Purpose Coach. Spiritual guidance & mediumship — all faiths welcome. EN & ES. Blessings and love.
  • Confirm credentials, exact mediumship wording, in-person vs. virtual, camera comfort, how much Spanish, and how he wants his faith named.
  • Confirm what he has for a domain, brand email, and Stripe. Invite brandon@gil.marketing as a user or admin. Booking and payments go under the active Un Momentico LLC.
  • Launch the bilingual website.
  • Film the first talking-head batch (with a simple filming guide) and approve the content already in review.
  • Pick the long-form format, name, and first guests; set up the recording; and record episode 1 alongside that first talking-head batch.
  • Collect verified, client-approved testimonials from clients he already has. Never invent them.
  • Launch initial marketing efforts: first posts go live, and the steady weekly cadence starts.

Days 31–60 · Review, Refine, Consider Paid

  • Review the first month of data. Double down on the topics and hooks that lead to real session inquiries.
  • Keep the weekly filming-and-publishing cadence; add a Spanish or Spanglish piece if he has agreed to a mix.
  • Publish the long-form sitting monthly and clip it into the weekly mix. Podcast-guest outreach complements this (he visits other shows; guests can also sit with him).
  • Refine the booking process, FAQ, and follow-up from real prospect questions.
  • Launch or grow the email letter (monthly, or the cadence he prefers).
  • Consider / test paid ads on Meta only once the organic cadence and the booking path are working. No spend is committed here; any test is a conversation, not a default.
  • Continue outreach: first guest and partnership conversations.
  • Switch on the Growth & Authority options you want (directories, review routine, speaking kit, first pitches).

Days 61–90 · Evaluate and Grow

  • Lock what earned real inquiries, and retire what did not, from the measured 90-day baseline — not guesses.
  • Grow the letter, and ship a larger campaign from a series that is already working.
  • Evaluate a move to two long-form sittings a month, and consider audio podcast distribution (Spotify, Apple).
  • Decide any other channels worth more weight (a stronger LinkedIn rhythm, or a first small in-person gathering).
  • Deepen the partnerships that responded, and plan the next quarter’s conference and speaking presence.
  • Keep Growth & Authority running; try a guest article or a small live session if it fits. The long-term line is a conversation, not a 90-day deliverable.
  • Set the next quarter’s targets from real numbers.

Built into the schedule, not a side list. Nothing goes out until it is approved. The letter stays separate from outreach. Days 61–90

  • Appear where booked. Deepen the partnerships that said yes.
  • Plan the next quarter’s in-person presence, and capture From the road reflections when he travels.

Growth & Authority — what we can switch on

From month 2 onward, once the foundation is live. These are options, not promises of coverage, rankings, or results, and no spend is committed here. Nothing is submitted or sent until it is approved.

Findable in the right places

  • Directory listings and backlinks — submitted through our form system, with a placement tracker. Spiritual, coaching, wellness, Hispanic-business, and South Florida local directories.
  • Google review routine — a simple ask after a session, only with the client’s permission.

Press, speaking, and writing

  • Press and expert quotes — journalist-request services, plus Spanish-language local TV, radio, and papers.
  • Speaking page and one-sheet — pitched to conferences, churches, retreats, grief and wellness events, and corporate lunch-and-learns.
  • Guest articles on wellness and faith blogs.

In the room, and later

  • Small live group sessions or workshops when he is ready.
  • Simple home recording setup for his space, free or low-cost, planned with him after launch.
  • Long term: a short bilingual book or guide; a workshop or course; and consistent name and profile information so he is easier to find in AI search.
