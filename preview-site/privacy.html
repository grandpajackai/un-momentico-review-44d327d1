What sessions are

Sessions with Danny Cabrera and Un Momentico Coaching Group are for spiritual and personal insight. They are not medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal or financial services, and they are not a substitute for care from a licensed professional. Please don’t stop or change any treatment because of something said in a session.

No guaranteed outcomes

Every session is different. No specific message, contact with a particular person, or result is promised. Messages are shared in good faith and are yours to interpret.

If you are in crisis

If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, US) or call 911.

Your privacy

What you share in a session stays between you and Danny. Information you send through this website is used only to reply to you and arrange your session. It is never sold, and testimonials are only published with your written permission.
