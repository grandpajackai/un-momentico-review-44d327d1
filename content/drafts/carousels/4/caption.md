## Carousel 4 — 4 questions to find the gifts already placed in you

**Pillar:** Purpose and God-given gifts  
**Goal:** High-save value post. A tool people come back to.  
**Slides:** `carousels/4/slide-01.png` … (7 slides)

### Slides

1. COVER — You already have gifts. Four questions to hear them. / Grab a pen. Answer honestly, not impressively. (kicker: Save this for a quiet evening)
2. Question 1/4: What do people already come to you for? — Advice, calm, a laugh, a plan? Write down the last three times someone leaned on you.
3. Question 2/4: When do you lose track of time, in a good way? — Not the scrolling kind. The kind where you look up and feel more like yourself.
4. Question 3/4: What breaks your heart enough to make you act? — Your gifts often live right next to what you can't stand to watch happen.
5. Question 4/4: What leaves you at peace afterwards, not just excited during? — Excitement fades. Peace is usually the better compass.
6. “Discern, then proceed with more light and love.” — Danny's daily reflection / Circle the answer that showed up twice. Take one small step with it this week.
7. CTA — Which question stopped you? Tell me the number in the comments. I read every one. Follow @realDancab. Blessings and love. + disclaimer

### Caption

Knowing that we all have God given gifts... most of us still struggle to name ours.

These four questions help. Grab a pen, answer honestly (not impressively), and look for the answer that shows up twice.

Then do what I always come back to: discern, then proceed with more light and love.
