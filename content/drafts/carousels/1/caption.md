## Carousel 1 — What a first session actually feels like

**Goal:** Remove the fear of booking a first session. Save + share.

### Slides

1. COVER — Your first session, minute by minute. / No script. No rush. Nothing to perform. (kicker: If you've been curious but nervous)
2. You don't have to prepare anything. — Come as you are: Tired, hopeful, sceptical. All of it is welcome.; Bring one question, if you have one: Or none. Sometimes the question shows up halfway through.; You don't need to share my faith: Only to arrive open.

6. One honest next step. Not ten. — Something to sit with: A question, a word, a feeling worth paying attention to.; One small step: Something you can actually do this week.; No pressure to come back: If it helped, you'll know.
7. CTA — Save this for the day you're ready. Or send it to someone who has been wondering what a session is like. Follow @realDancab. Blessings and love. + disclaimer
