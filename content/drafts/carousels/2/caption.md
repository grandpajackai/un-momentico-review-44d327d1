## Carousel 2 — 5 things people get wrong about mediumship

**Pillar:** Grief and connection / myth-busting  
**Goal:** Make mediumship approachable and honest. Saves + shares to someone grieving.  
**Slides:** `carousels/2/slide-01.png` … (7 slides)

### Slides

1. COVER — 5 things people get wrong about mediumship. / From someone who holds this work with care. (kicker: Gently, and honestly)
2. Myth 1: “It's a show.” → What’s true: It's a quiet conversation. No candles required, no theatrics. Just two people listening carefully.
3. Myth 2: “You have to believe what I believe.” → What’s true: My faith is my story, not your requirement. People of every faith, and of none, are welcome. I only ask that you arrive open.
4. Myth 3: “The person you hope for will always come through.” → What’s true: I never promise a specific message or outcome. When something comes, I share it with humility. When it doesn't, we still sit with what's on your heart.
5. Myth 4: “It replaces grieving, or your counselor.” → What’s true: It's for spiritual and personal insight. Keep your doctor, your counselor and your people. This can sit alongside them.
6. Myth 5: “It's only about the people who have passed.” → What’s true: Often the most important part is about you: how to carry the love forward, and how to live now.
7. CTA — Carrying someone with you today? Save this, or send it to someone who is. Questions are welcome in my DMs. Follow @realDancab. Blessings and love. + disclaimer
